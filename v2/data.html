Data

Download everything behind the map as a spreadsheet: one row for each of New Jersey's 564 municipalities.

All of this data comes from public sources. Each value is either taken directly from a published source (the U.S. Census Bureau, HUD, the NJ Department of Community Affairs, Freddie Mac, the National Zoning and Land Use Database) or calculated from those values using the formulas in the methodology. OpenHousing does not estimate, fill in or generate any numbers. Where a source has no value for a town, the cell is left empty.

What the spreadsheet holds

One row per municipality, identified by its official Census ID and New Jersey municipal code. The columns fall into these groups:

GroupWhat it includesFrom
TownName, type, county, population, householdsCensus Bureau, NJOGIS
OwningTypical home value and where it came from, yearly mortgage payment, average property tax bill, yearly ownership costCensus Bureau, Freddie Mac, NJ DCA
RentingMedian rent, share of renters paying 30% or more of income, HUD Fair Market RentCensus Bureau, HUD
IncomeTown and county median household income; HUD's moderate-income limit for a family of fourCensus Bureau, HUD
Measures and scoresCost as a share of income, the ownership and rental scores, ranks and percentilesCalculated from the rows above
ReliabilityConfidence flag and reason for each score, Census margins of error, and flags where the Census reports only "at least" a valueCensus Bureau; calculated
Barriers (not scored)Zoning restrictiveness (105 towns), Fourth Round present and prospective need, Qualified Urban Aid statusNZLUD, NJ DCA

Every row also records the data period of its home value, the mortgage rate used, and the tax year. The full list of sources, with their data periods and terms, is on the Sources page.

Good to know

  • Empty means unavailable. Small towns sometimes have no Census estimate; those cells are blank, never zero.
  • Check the confidence column. Estimates for small towns come from small survey samples and can be far off.
  • Obligations are the state's calculation from October 2024, not records of homes built.
  • The data refreshes monthly. The file below is always the latest version that passed every check.
  • Credit the sources when you use the data, as listed on the Sources page.
