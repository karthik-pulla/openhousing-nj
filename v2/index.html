Housing affordability for every town in New Jersey

OpenHousing is a free, open tool that shows how affordable it is to own or rent a home in each of New Jersey's 564 municipalities.

Open the map How it's calculated

What it does

  • Scores every town on owning and rentingTwo 0–100 scores, so you can see where housing costs take the smallest and largest share of what people in the region earn.
  • Explains each town in plain EnglishHome values, rents, property taxes, a price history and a short summary, with the source of every number one hover away.
  • Lets you compare and downloadPut up to three towns side by side, or download the full dataset or a single town as a spreadsheet.
  • Shows the barriers, tooZoning rules and the state's affordable-housing obligations, shown as context. They never change a score.

How it does it

  1. Collects public data every monthFrom the U.S. Census Bureau, HUD, the NJ Department of Community Affairs and Freddie Mac, matched to towns by official ID.
  2. Compares costs with regional incomeThe yearly cost of owning (mortgage plus property tax) or renting the typical home, divided by the county's median household income, then ranked across all towns.
  3. Flags uncertainty and checks the resultEach score says how reliable it is. Nothing is published unless all 564 towns are present and the numbers pass checks. Missing data stays missing.
