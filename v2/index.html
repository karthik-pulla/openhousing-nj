Which New Jersey towns are actually affordable?

What it costs to own or rent the typical home in each of the state's 564 towns, compared with what households in the region earn. Every number shows its source.

          How it works

          1. 1

            Gather

            Public data from the Census Bureau, HUD, the NJ Department of Community Affairs and Freddie Mac, refreshed monthly and matched to towns by official ID.

          2. 2

            Measure

            Yearly housing costs divided by county median income, ranked across all towns as a 0–100 score with a confidence flag.

          3. 3

            Check

            Published only after validation: all 564 towns present, no unexpected gaps, values in plausible ranges.

          Zoning rules and the state's affordable-housing obligations are shown alongside the scores as context. They never change a score.
